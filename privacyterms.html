Privacy Policy

This page explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you browse our website or purchase products from our store.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we may collect the following types of information from you:

  • Personal Information: Your name, email address, phone number, billing address, and shipping address.
  • Order Information: The shirts you purchase, selected sizes, colors, quantities, and order history.
  • Payment Information: Payment details processed through our payment providers. We do not directly store complete payment card details unless explicitly stated.
  • Technical Information: Your IP address, browser type, device information, and website activity, which may be collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders.
  • Arrange shipping and provide order updates.
  • Respond to inquiries and customer support requests.
  • Improve our website, products, and shopping experience.
  • Send promotional emails or announcements when permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.
  • Detect fraudulent transactions and maintain website security.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share relevant information with trusted third parties that help us operate our business, including payment processors, shipping and delivery providers, website hosting services, and customer support platforms.

We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect our legal rights.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to remember your preferences, maintain shopping cart functionality, understand website traffic, and improve your browsing experience.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, certain website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online transaction or data storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing activities, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights or raise a privacy concern, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

Terms & Conditions

This page outlines the rules and guidelines for using our website, purchasing products, making payments, and handling shipping, returns, and refunds.

1. Use of Our Website

You agree to use our website for lawful purposes and to provide accurate, complete information when placing an order. You must not misuse the website, attempt to disrupt its operation, or engage in fraudulent activities.

2. Products and Descriptions

We make reasonable efforts to display accurate product descriptions, colors, sizes, and images. However, actual colors may vary depending on your screen or device.

Please review product descriptions and size charts carefully before ordering. Product availability, designs, and specifications may change without prior notice.

3. Pricing and Payments

All prices are displayed in the currency indicated on our website. Prices, discounts, and promotional offers may change at any time, but changes will not affect orders that have already been confirmed, except where correction or cancellation is legally permitted.

Orders must be paid through the payment methods available at checkout. We reserve the right to cancel or refuse an order in cases of suspected fraud, pricing errors, or product unavailability, subject to applicable law. If payment has already been collected for a canceled order, an appropriate refund will be provided.

4. Order and Order Confirmation

After placing an order, you should receive an order confirmation through the contact details you provided. An order confirmation acknowledges receipt of your order but does not necessarily guarantee acceptance until the order has been reviewed and processed.

Please ensure that your shipping address, contact details, selected sizes, and quantities are correct before completing your purchase.

5. Shipping and Delivery

We aim to process and ship orders within the estimated time stated on our website. Delivery times are estimates and may be affected by courier delays, weather conditions, holidays, or other circumstances beyond our control.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate delivery information. Additional shipping fees or arrangements may apply if an order cannot be delivered because of incorrect or incomplete details, subject to applicable law.

6. Returns, Exchanges, and Refunds

Customers may request a return, exchange, or refund for eligible orders in accordance with our posted return policy and applicable consumer protection laws.

If an item arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or significantly different from its description, please contact us with your order number and supporting photographs, where appropriate.

Items must meet the applicable return conditions. Any exclusions, including those for customized products, must be disclosed clearly and will not override rights provided by law.

Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method where possible. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider.
